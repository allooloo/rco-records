RCO: proof of every AI agent read.

Allooloo saw the gap first because of where it sits. It carries 100M AI agent traffic a month, among the highest in finance worldwide. Watching that many agents work, call after call, across every market, showed what none of them left behind: proof. That edge is what let Allooloo spot the need and build the next generation of record-keeping for AI agents. The receipt is made inside the read itself, so the audit trail exists the moment the agent acts, and the firm, its auditor and its regulator can each check it.

Capital markets keep a record of everything a person does: the order ticket, the recorded call, the trade confirmation. An AI agent had no equivalent. It could read an issuer, a filing or a rule and act on it, and the only trace was a log its own firm wrote. No third party could say what the agent read, when, or under which market's rules. With RCO, one can.

§1 WHAT RCO IS

One receipt per cleared read

RCO — the Record of Cleared Orders — is the record of what an AI agent relied on. When an Allooloo gate clears a read, it writes one receipt to this chain: what was read, in which market, under which rule version, when, and the hash of the answer the agent received. The receipt is signed by Allooloo and chained to the one before it.

When a regulator, a client or an auditor asks what an AI agent relied on, a firm's own logs are its own word. A receipt is a third party's: signed, dated and impossible to alter after the fact. That is the traceability and explainability a model risk team asks for, for agents in session and for the agents a firm designs, builds and runs at enterprise grade.

Paired with Allooloo's data, a firm holds both halves: the golden dataset its agents read from, and the receipt that proves they did.

Receipts are written per customer key and per subject type; no cross-reading.

  • MCP: https://mcp.rco-records.ai/mcp — Model Context Protocol (MCP), streamable HTTP, stateless, JSON-RPC 2.0, typed errors.
  • verify_receipt — Check any receipt by its id: the hash, the signature and its place in the chain. Public, no key needed.
  • list_receipts — The audit trail of a firm's own AI agents: every cleared read, in order, under the firm's key.
  • get_statement — A signed statement of a firm's receipts for a period, for books and records and for its auditor.
  • issue_auditor_key — A firm gives its auditor a key to read and verify the firm's receipts independently.
  • write_receipt — Called by Allooloo's own MCPs when a read is cleared. One receipt per cleared read.
  • Operator: Allooloo Technologies Corp.. One writer: Allooloo's gates.

§2 RECEIPT V1 — THE FROZEN SPEC

Canonical JSON, keys sorted, UTF-8, no whitespace

One receipt per cleared read; writes are idempotent on (writer, subject, subject_type, market, rule_version, cleared_at, answer_hash). Target size under 600 bytes. Fields are only ever added, never renamed or removed; unknown fields are ignored by readers.

FieldMeaning
v1
idrco: + 20-char base32 of the first 100 bits of hash. Derived, never assigned.
seqMonotonic integer across the whole chain.
writerThe gate that wrote it (allooloo).
callerWho asked: a customer key id, or an x402 wallet handle. Stored as sha256 of the value. Empty when unknown.
subjectWhat was read: the identifier (ISIN, LEI, …).
subject_typeisin | lei | gtin | … — open list.
marketISO country or exchange code of the read.
rule_versionVersion of the rule set the gate cleared under.
cleared_atRFC 3339 UTC, seconds.
answer_hashsha256 of the canonical answer bytes the agent received.
prev_hashhash of the previous receipt (seq − 1).
hashsha256 over the canonical receipt without hash, sig (and id, which is derived from it).
sigEd25519 over hash, by kid.
kidKey id, resolvable at /.well-known/jwks.json.

§3 VERIFY

verify(receipt, jwks) is a pure function

Recompute id and hash, check sig against kid. A receipt verifies forever without asking Allooloo anything. That is non-repudiation in practice: the proof stands on its own, whoever is asking and whenever they ask. Reference implementations in TypeScript, Python and Go, and the spec, under the MIT licence: github.com/allooloo/rco-records.

  • https://rco-records.ai/receipts/{id} — the same answer as verify_receipt, over GET, for any client.
  • Keyring: /.well-known/jwks.json — rco-allooloo-2026-10 (signing), and the two historical keys the v0 receipts name.
